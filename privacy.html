CaseKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

CaseKeeper helps you organize personal cases, disputes, service issues, deadlines, events, contacts, evidence, notes, and related documentation. This Privacy Policy explains how CaseKeeper handles information when you use the app.

Information You Add

CaseKeeper allows you to enter information such as:

  • Case titles and categories
  • Reference numbers and summaries
  • Contact and organization information
  • Deadlines and events
  • Notes and case updates
  • Photos, screenshots, scanned receipts, PDFs, and other supported document files

You decide what information to enter. Do not enter information that you are not comfortable storing on your device or sharing through another app.

CaseKeeper does not require an account, password, Social Security number, financial account credentials, or other sensitive information.

Local Storage

CaseKeeper is designed to store your case information and evidence locally on your device. CaseKeeper does not operate a remote account database or upload your case content to its own servers.

Information stored on your device may be included in device backups or Apple-managed services depending on your iOS, Apple ID, iCloud, and backup settings. CaseKeeper does not control Apple’s backup or cloud services.

Photos, Camera, Scanning, and Files

If you choose to add evidence, CaseKeeper may request access to your camera, Photos library, or Files app.

These permissions allow you to:

  • Take photographs
  • Scan documents
  • Select photos
  • Select PDFs and other supported files
  • Attach documentation to a case

CaseKeeper does not access your photos, camera, or files unless you choose to use one of these features and grant permission through iOS.

Evidence you add is stored with the related case on your device.

Calendar Features

CaseKeeper may allow you to add case deadlines to Apple Calendar.

Calendar access is optional. CaseKeeper requests Calendar permission only when you choose to add deadlines to Apple Calendar. If permission is granted, CaseKeeper may create calendar events for the deadlines you select.

CaseKeeper does not use calendar information for advertising, tracking, or marketing. CaseKeeper does not provide a separate reminder or notification service.

Calendar events remain subject to Apple’s system services and your device settings.

PDF Reports and Sharing

CaseKeeper may allow you to generate a case report containing information you chose to include, such as case details, summaries, deadlines, events, notes, contacts, attached evidence, and document files.

When you export or share a report using AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files, or another app, the information is sent according to your instructions and handled by iOS and the receiving app or service.

After you share or export information, CaseKeeper cannot control how the receiving person, app, or service stores or uses it.

Purchases

CaseKeeper may offer a one-time in-app purchase called CaseKeeper Unlimited. The current price is US $0.99, although the displayed price may vary by country, currency, taxes, or Apple’s storefront.

Purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit services. CaseKeeper does not receive or store your full payment-card information.

Apple may process transaction information necessary to complete purchases, restore purchases, and verify your entitlement.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

CaseKeeper does not currently use:

  • Third-party advertising
  • Third-party analytics services
  • Cross-app tracking
  • Behavioral advertising
  • Data brokers
  • Advertising identifiers for marketing

CaseKeeper does not sell your case information or personal information.

Information Sharing

CaseKeeper does not sell, rent, or license your case information.

CaseKeeper does not share your case content with third parties unless:

  • You choose to export or share it
  • You save it to another app or service
  • You use Apple system features such as Calendar, Photos, Files, AirDrop, Mail, or Messages
  • Disclosure is required by law or legal process
  • It is necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of the app or its users

Data Retention and Deletion

Your case information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

You can delete cases, events, deadlines, contacts, and evidence using the deletion features provided in CaseKeeper.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored CaseKeeper data, subject to iOS behavior and device backups. Exported reports, shared files, email attachments, AirDrop copies, and files saved outside CaseKeeper are not deleted when you delete information inside the app.

Because CaseKeeper does not require an account, there is no CaseKeeper online account to delete.

Security

CaseKeeper relies on iOS security and file-protection features where available. You should protect your device with a strong passcode and Apple’s available security features.

No app can guarantee absolute security. Your information may be exposed if your device is unlocked, shared, compromised, backed up insecurely, or if you export or share files with another person or service.

Not Legal Advice

CaseKeeper is an organization and documentation tool. It does not provide legal advice, financial advice, professional advice, or representation.

CaseKeeper does not guarantee any particular result in a dispute, complaint, claim, service issue, legal matter, or other case.

You are responsible for reviewing the accuracy of your information, meeting deadlines, protecting confidential information, and deciding how and where to share your documentation.

Children’s Privacy

CaseKeeper is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through CaseKeeper.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
