CaseKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 6, 2026

CaseKeeper is designed to help users organize personal cases, disputes, service issues, deadlines, evidence, notes, and related documentation. Your privacy is important, and this policy explains how CaseKeeper handles information.

Information You Enter

CaseKeeper may allow you to enter case titles, categories, other party names, reference numbers, summaries, timeline notes, deadlines, photos, screenshots, scanned receipts, and other evidence or documentation that you choose to add.

This information is created and controlled by you. CaseKeeper does not require you to enter sensitive personal information, passwords, financial account credentials, Social Security numbers, or legal documents. If you choose to enter sensitive information, you are responsible for deciding what information is appropriate to store in the app.

Local Storage

CaseKeeper is designed to store your case information locally on your device. The app does not sell your data and does not use your case information for advertising.

Information stored on your device may also be included in device backups depending on your iOS settings, Apple ID settings, iCloud backup settings, and device configuration.

Photos, Files, and Evidence

If you choose to attach evidence, CaseKeeper may request access to photos, files, camera, or related system features. These permissions are used only to let you add evidence that you choose to attach to a case.

CaseKeeper does not access your photos or files unless you choose to use those features and grant permission through iOS.

Calendar and Reminder Features

CaseKeeper may allow you to track deadlines and optionally add deadlines to Apple Calendar or use local notifications/reminders. These features are optional and are only used when you choose to enable them.

Calendar entries, reminders, and notifications are handled through Apple’s system services and your device settings.

PDF Export and Sharing

CaseKeeper may allow you to export a case report as a PDF or document. Exported reports may include case details, timelines, deadlines, notes, and evidence references that you chose to include in the case.

When you share or export a report through AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files, or another app, that sharing is controlled by you and handled by iOS and the receiving app or service.

Purchases

CaseKeeper may offer an optional paid upgrade or in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. CaseKeeper does not receive or store your full payment card information.

Analytics and Advertising

CaseKeeper does not currently use third-party advertising, third-party tracking, or third-party analytics services.

Data Sharing

CaseKeeper does not sell your personal information. CaseKeeper does not share your case content with third parties unless you choose to export, share, send, or store that information using another app, service, or Apple system feature.

Data Deletion

You can delete case information from within the app where deletion features are provided. You may also remove the app from your device. Deleting the app may remove locally stored app data from the device, subject to iOS behavior, backups, and any files you previously exported or shared.

Security

CaseKeeper uses iOS device security features where available. You should protect your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. No app can guarantee absolute security, especially if a device is unlocked, shared, compromised, backed up, or if exported files are sent outside the app.

Not Legal Advice

CaseKeeper is an organization and documentation tool. It does not provide legal advice, financial advice, professional advice, or guarantee any particular outcome in a dispute, claim, complaint, or legal matter.

Children’s Privacy

CaseKeeper is not intended for children under 13. The app is intended for users who can responsibly manage their own documentation and personal information.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
